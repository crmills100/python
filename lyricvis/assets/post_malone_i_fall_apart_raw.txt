Now the pain lived under his skin. It burned in the pit of his stomach, in his lungs when he tried to breathe too deep. Scarred. That’s what he was now. He knew it. And yet, he couldn’t stop hoping she’d call.

A stoplight turned red. He slammed the brakes. The car jerked to a halt. Outside, the rain hit the windshield like a thousand tiny apologies. Inside, his tears finally fell. No shame. No one to see.

He whispered into the silence, “You said we'd be together.”

Only the storm answered.
